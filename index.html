About

Gilt Edge Atlas tells little-known historical mysteries from the documents themselves: expedition journals, period newspapers, archive photographs and maps. Facts come first, and theories are labelled as theories.

The studio publishes on YouTube (@giltedgeatlas), TikTok (@giltedgeatlas) and Instagram. Full documentaries are published on YouTube; TikTok and Instagram receive short vertical excerpts.

ULP Studio Publisher

ULP Studio Publisher is the studio's internal tool. It uploads the studio's own finished videos to the studio's own social media accounts after the owner approves each one. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

What we collect

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, Google (YouTube) or Meta when the owner connects the studio's own accounts.
  • Basic profile information of those accounts (account ID, display name) and the status of uploaded videos.
  • Public statistics of the studio's own videos (views, likes), used to plan future videos.

The tool does not collect data about viewers or other users.

How we use and store it

The data is used only to publish the studio's videos and read their statistics. Tokens are kept on the studio's private server with restricted file permissions. They are not shared, sold or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects an account. Access can be revoked at any time in the security settings of TikTok, Google or Meta; the studio then deletes the stored tokens.
